Structural Engineering  ·  Earthquake Engineering  ·  Structural Dynamics

Civil and structural engineer (Ph.D. candidate, UC San Diego) with over six years of hands-on graduate-level experience in the planning, design, construction, instrumentation, and execution of large-scale engineering projects, including full-scale, multi-story shake-table tests of the tallest cold-formed steel and mass-timber buildings ever tested. Experienced in working with multidisciplinary teams of researchers, technicians, contractors, and industry partners to take complex infrastructure from concept through construction and operation, and in analyzing large datasets to quantify system performance and deliver clear, evidence-based recommendations. Skilled in nonlinear finite element modeling (ABAQUS), system identification, and programming (MATLAB, Python) to develop efficient, standardized analysis and design workflows, and familiar with code- and standards-based design and performance-based design of buildings and nonstructural components. Contributor to three major testing programs: CFS10, NHERI TallWood, and Converging Design. Recipient of the 2026 ASCE Norman Medal.

Seeking full-time opportunities in structural engineering design and consulting, with particular interests in nonlinear structural analysis, seismic assessment, and forensic engineering.
